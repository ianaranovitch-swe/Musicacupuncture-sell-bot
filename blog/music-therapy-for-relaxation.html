Music therapy for relaxation

Draft guide · Meditation music · Stress relief

Music therapy in a wellness sense means using sound on purpose: to unwind, focus, or sleep better. With Musicacupuncture, you can buy healing music MP3 files and build a short listening habit — ideal if you want music for relaxation and stress relief without complicated setup.

Simple listening tips

  • Pick a quiet window: morning, lunch break, or before bed
  • Start with 10–20 minutes of meditation music at moderate volume
  • Prefer headphones when you want immersion; speakers for ambient calm
  • Repeat the same track for a few days so your body learns the cue to relax
