What is Music Acupuncture?

Draft guide · Musicacupuncture · Music therapy

Music Acupuncture (also written Musicacupuncture) is a therapeutic music concept created by Michael B. Johnsson. It blends carefully crafted sound frequencies with ideas inspired by acupuncture meridians and traditional energetic practices. The goal is simple: music that supports relaxation, meditation, and a sense of inner balance in everyday life.

Music therapy meets meridian-inspired sound

Classical acupuncture works with pathways often called meridians. Music Acupuncture does not replace needles or clinical care. Instead, it offers healing music and sound healing listening experiences designed around calm, clarity, and energy balance — so you can create a gentle daily ritual with headphones or speakers.

Who it is for

  • People looking for music for relaxation and stress relief
  • Listeners who enjoy meditation music and frequency-focused tracks
  • Anyone curious about music for meridians as a wellness companion
